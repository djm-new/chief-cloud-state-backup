# Weekly editorial calibration

Session lesson: the weekly podcast-of-podcasts failed when it behaved like a compressed digest instead of an editorial synthesis.

## What the weekly script must do

- Prefer *argument extraction* over recap.
- For each finalist, identify:
  - the main claim;
  - the best supporting evidence or example;
  - the key counterargument or ambiguity;
  - why the conversation matters to DJ specifically.
- Synthesize across episodes around themes, tensions, and tradeoffs.
- If the result feels too short, expand the analysis rather than compressing harder.

## What to avoid

- Thin "this episode is about X" summaries.
- Generic "valuable conversation" phrasing without specific stakes.
- Audio that sounds like one narrator reading a bulletin.

## Voice calibration notes

- Two-host audio needs clearly different voice identities, not just small rate changes.
- If voices sound fake or mechanical, fix voice selection and speaking structure first.
- Dialogue should sound conversational and reactive, with brief turns and natural pauses.

## Success criteria

- The transcript should read like an editor's notes for intelligent listeners.
- The audio should sound like a real two-host show, not a stitched summary.
- The final artifact should surface what is interesting, contested, or newly important—not merely what happened.
