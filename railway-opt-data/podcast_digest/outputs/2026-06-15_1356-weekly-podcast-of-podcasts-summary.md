# Weekly Podcast-of-Podcasts — Summary

## Top line
This week’s strongest thread was that AI is moving from model novelty to *infrastructure, supply-chain, and adoption economics*.

## What made the cut
- **Jensen Huang / Training Data** — AI as the next computing stack: not just chips, but factories, energy, and generation.
- **Dan Dreyfus / All-In** — critical minerals as an AI bottleneck; upstream materials may matter as much as model quality.
- **Rebecca Hinds / The Cognitive Revolution** — workers are using AI, but many are just “babysitting” it, so productivity gains are still fragile.
- **Stratechery / Ben Thompson** — Anthropic’s Fable / AI tiers / safety-vs-utility tension remains a major strategic signal.
- **a16z / healthcare AI** — healthcare is a real proving ground, but adoption is still slowed by workflow, compliance, and governance.

## DJ takeaway
The useful lens is no longer “who has the coolest demo?”
It’s:
- who owns the infrastructure,
- who controls the bottlenecks,
- who can convert usage into real productivity,
- and who can ship useful AI without breaking trust.
