### Episode 2: **All-In Podcast** – *Dan Dreyfus: America's Critical Minerals Crisis is Here*

MAYA: Dan Dreyfus outlines the looming crisis in critical minerals supply, driven by China’s dominance and infrastructure bottlenecks. He warns that the U.S. is dangerously unprepared for the commodity supercycle driven by AI, EVs, and clean energy.

SAM: The strongest argument is that critical minerals like lithium, cobalt, and rare earths are the new oil—strategic assets that determine technological and military power. Dreyfus argues that the U.S. lacks both domestic production capacity and secure supply chains.

MAYA: A counterpoint: Could recycling, substitution, or new mining technologies offset the current dependency on China? It’s unclear how quickly these alternatives can scale to meet demand.

SAM: Why should DJ care? This is a strategic risk in the AI and energy transition story. If the U.S. can’t secure critical minerals, it risks falling behind in both AI infrastructure and green energy, with cascading effects on national security and tech leadership.

### Episode 3: **The Cognitive Revolution** – *Babysitting the Machine: Glean's Rebecca Hinds*

MAYA: Rebecca Hinds from Glean reveals that while 87% of workers use AI, only 13% report improved performance—largely due to “botsitting” and “botshitting.” Hidden labor costs are undermining AI’s promised productivity gains.

SAM: The strongest evidence: A large portion of AI use in the enterprise is actually spent managing, correcting, and babysitting AI outputs rather than gaining efficiency. This contradicts the narrative that AI is already a productivity multiplier.

MAYA: A counterpoint: This may be a temporary phase. As models improve and interfaces evolve, the friction may decrease, and productivity gains could materialize.

SAM: Why should DJ care? This challenges the bullish AI productivity thesis. Investors and operators need to be cautious about overestimating near-term ROI from AI tools in the enterprise, especially without better workflows and user training.
