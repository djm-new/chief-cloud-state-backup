The episode dives into far-UVC germicidal light at 222nm, a technology that can inactivate airborne pathogens without harming human skin. The guests explain how this light can function like a powerful air purifier in shared spaces and could be a critical tool in preventing future pandemics. The conversation highlights high-impact deployment areas such as schools, transport hubs, and long-term care facilities.
